MariBank Philippines (formerly SeaBank Philippines) offers a mobile banking application that enables you to manage your finances seamlessly on your mobile phone, anytime and anywhere. You can send and receive transactions from both MariBank and non-MariBank users, all while earning competitive interest rates with no additional fees and no minimum balance. MariBank is part of Sea Limited (NYSE:SE), a global consumer internet company founded and headquartered in Singapore and listed on the New York Stock Exchange. It operates three core businesses across digital entertainment, e-commerce, as well as digital payments and financial services, known as Garena, Shopee, and Monee respectively.
